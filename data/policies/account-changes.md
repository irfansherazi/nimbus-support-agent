## Identity verification before account changes

Before the Nimbus support assistant can view or change any account details, the customer must verify their identity. Identity verification needs two pieces of information:

- The Nimbus account ID, which starts with C- followed by digits, for example C-1001. The account ID is shown in the workspace admin settings under Account, and at the top of every invoice.
- The 4-digit support PIN for the account. The account owner sets the support PIN in the workspace admin settings under Account, then Security.

Both the account ID and the support PIN must match for verification to succeed. The support assistant never asks for a password, a two-factor code or card details, and customers should never share them in a support conversation.

## Failed verification attempts

Customers have three attempts to verify their identity in a single support conversation. After three failed verification attempts, verification is locked for the rest of that conversation, and the support assistant cannot verify the customer or make account changes in it. A customer who is locked out of verification can start a new conversation later, or open a support ticket so a human specialist can confirm their identity by other means. A customer who has forgotten the support PIN can reset it from the workspace admin settings after signing in to Nimbus.

## Changes the support assistant can make

Once a customer is verified, the Nimbus support assistant can update the following account details directly:

- The account email address, which is used for sign-in and account notices.
- The account phone number, which is used for account recovery and for Enterprise phone support callbacks.
- The billing contact, which is the name and email address that receive invoices and payment notices.

Changes to the account email, phone number or billing contact take effect immediately. Nimbus sends a confirmation notice to both the old and the new email address when the account email changes, so that the owner is alerted if the change was not expected. The support assistant confirms the new value with the customer before saving the change.

## Changes that need a human specialist

Some account changes have billing, legal or security consequences and are handled only by a human Nimbus specialist through a support ticket. The support assistant cannot make these changes, but it can open the support ticket on the customer's behalf. Changes that need a human specialist are:

- Plan changes, including upgrades from Free to Pro or Pro to Enterprise, and downgrades to a lower plan.
- Account ownership transfer, which moves the owner role and billing responsibility to another person.
- Account deletion, which permanently removes the account and its data after the retention period.

A specialist normally replies to the support ticket within the response time for the workspace's plan, as set out in the Support Hours and SLA policy.

## Plan changes

Plan changes are processed by a billing specialist. When a workspace upgrades, the new plan starts immediately and the price difference is prorated on the next invoice. When a workspace downgrades, the change takes effect at the end of the current billing period. A downgrade from Enterprise is governed by the customer's contract and the notice terms in the Cancellation Policy. Customers should include their account ID and the plan they want in the support ticket.

## Account ownership transfer

An account ownership transfer moves the owner role of a Nimbus workspace to another user. The current owner must request the transfer through a support ticket, and the new owner must already be a member of the workspace and confirm the transfer by email. If the current owner is no longer available, for example because they have left the organisation, the specialist asks for written authorisation from the organisation before transferring ownership. Ownership transfers usually complete within 2 business days of all confirmations being received.

## Account deletion

Account deletion permanently removes a Nimbus account, all its workspaces and all its data. Account deletion is requested through a support ticket and confirmed by a specialist with the account owner. After deletion is confirmed, data is kept for 30 days and then permanently deleted, as described in the Data Privacy Policy. Customers should export their data before requesting deletion.
