## How Nimbus refunds work

The Nimbus Refund Policy sets out when a charge on a Nimbus invoice can be refunded. Refund eligibility depends on the workspace's plan, the amount requested, and how long ago the invoice was issued. Each plan's limit is stated in its own section below. Every refund is tied to a specific invoice, identified by its invoice ID (for example, INV-2207), so customers should have the invoice ID ready when asking for a refund.

## Free plan {tier=free}

Free plan charges are not eligible for refunds. The Free plan itself costs $0, but Free plan workspaces can buy add-ons such as extra storage. Add-ons bought on a Free plan workspace count as Free plan charges, so Free plan add-on charges cannot be refunded. A Free plan customer who no longer wants an add-on can remove it from the workspace settings to stop future charges; the removal takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Pro plan {tier=pro}

Refunds of up to $200 are available within 30 days of the invoice date. The Pro plan refund limit applies per invoice and covers both monthly and annual Pro plan invoices. Pro plan annual invoices follow exactly the same limit as monthly invoices; paying annually does not increase the amount or the time allowed. A Pro plan refund request that is within the limit can be processed by the support assistant once the customer's identity has been verified. A Pro plan refund request above the limit, or for an invoice older than the limit allows, is passed to a billing specialist through a support ticket.

## Enterprise plan {tier=enterprise}

Refunds of up to $1,000 are available within 60 days of the invoice date. The Enterprise plan refund limit applies per invoice and covers both monthly and annual Enterprise invoices, including invoices paid by bank transfer on net-30 terms. Enterprise annual invoices follow exactly the same limit as monthly invoices. An Enterprise refund request that is within the limit can be processed by the support assistant once the customer's identity has been verified. An Enterprise refund request above the limit, or for an invoice older than the limit allows, is passed to a billing specialist through a support ticket, and the customer's customer success manager is copied on the ticket.

## Refund eligibility requirements

A refund can only be issued to an account with an active contract or subscription in good standing. Accounts that have expired, have been cancelled beyond their retention period, or are past due cannot receive refunds until the account issue is resolved. A past-due account must first pay its outstanding balance; once the account is back in good standing, the refund request can be reviewed under the normal limits for its plan.

A refund can never exceed the amount actually paid on the invoice being refunded. If an invoice was partly paid, partly credited, or already partly refunded, the maximum refund is the remaining paid amount on that invoice. Refunds are calculated per invoice and cannot be combined across several invoices to exceed a plan's limit.

## Refund processing time and payment method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method used to pay the invoice. Card refunds and bank refunds both arrive within 5 to 10 business days after approval, depending on the customer's bank. Nimbus cannot send a refund to a different card, a different bank account or a different person. If the original card has expired or been cancelled, the customer's bank normally forwards the refund to the replacement card; if it cannot, the customer should open a support ticket so a billing specialist can arrange the refund.

## Requests outside the standard limits

Refund requests that are larger than the plan's limit, or older than the plan's limit allows, are not automatically refused. They are escalated to a human billing specialist through a support ticket. The billing specialist reviews the account history and the reason for the request and replies by email. The support assistant cannot approve, promise or estimate the outcome of an escalated refund request. Opening a ticket does not change the refund rules stated in this Refund Policy.

## Annual plans and refunds

Annual plans are refunded under the same per-tier limit as monthly plans. There is no separate annual-plan refund amount and no prorated refund of unused months beyond what the Refund Policy allows for the workspace's plan. Customers who cancel an annual plan keep access until the end of the paid term, as described in the Cancellation Policy.
