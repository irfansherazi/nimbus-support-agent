## Billing cycle and invoices

Nimbus bills each workspace on a recurring billing date, which is the date the workspace first moved to a paid plan. Monthly plans are billed on the same day each month, and annual plans are billed once a year on the anniversary of that date. If the billing date falls on a day that does not exist in a given month, such as the 31st, the invoice is issued on the last day of that month.

Every Nimbus invoice is issued on the billing date and emailed to the workspace's billing contact. Each invoice has a unique invoice ID in the format INV followed by a number, for example INV-2207. Customers should quote the invoice ID when asking about a charge, a refund or a payment. Invoices list the plan, the number of users, any add-ons, prorated adjustments, taxes and the total due. Past invoices can be downloaded as PDF from the Billing page in the workspace admin settings.

## Payment methods

Nimbus accepts payment by credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard and American Express) on all paid plans. Card payments are charged automatically on the billing date. Enterprise customers can also choose to pay by invoice, as described in the Enterprise section below. Nimbus does not accept cash, cheques or payment through third-party marketplaces.

## Pro plan billing {tier=pro}

Pro plan workspaces are billed at $49 per user per month, or $490 per user per year on annual billing. Pro plan invoices are paid by card, which is charged automatically on the billing date. Users added during a billing period are charged on a prorated basis on the next invoice. Users removed during a billing period stay billed until the end of that period and drop off from the next invoice. Pro plan customers who switch from monthly to annual billing are charged the annual price on the switch date, with a prorated credit for the unused part of the current month.

## Enterprise plan billing {tier=enterprise}

Enterprise plan pricing is set in the customer's contract, and Enterprise workspaces can be billed monthly or annually. Enterprise customers can pay by card or by invoice. Invoice payment uses net-30 terms, meaning payment is due 30 calendar days after the invoice is issued, by bank transfer to the account details printed on the invoice. Enterprise customers can add a purchase order number to their invoices by asking their customer success manager or opening a support ticket. Seat true-ups and contract changes are billed as described in the contract.

## Taxes

Nimbus adds sales tax, VAT or GST to invoices where required by the customer's billing address. The tax rate is calculated from the billing country and, where relevant, the state or province. Business customers in the EU and UK can add a valid VAT number in the workspace billing settings; once the VAT number is verified, reverse charge applies and VAT is not added to future invoices. Tax is not recalculated on invoices issued before a VAT number was added. Tax-exempt organisations in the US can send an exemption certificate through a support ticket for review.

## Updating a payment method

Workspace owners and billing admins can update the card on file at any time from the Billing page in the workspace admin settings. The new card is used for the next charge and for any outstanding past-due balance. The support assistant cannot see, take or change card numbers; customers must enter card details themselves on the Billing page. The billing contact who receives invoices is separate from the payment method and can be changed by the support assistant once the customer's identity is verified, as described in the Account Changes Policy.

## Failed payments and past-due accounts

If a card payment fails, Nimbus retries the charge automatically on the 3rd, 7th and 10th day after the billing date and emails the billing contact after each failed attempt. An account becomes past due when an invoice is unpaid after its due date. Past-due accounts get a 14-day grace period from the due date, during which the workspace works normally. If the balance is still unpaid after the 14-day grace period, the workspace becomes read-only: users can view and download files but cannot upload, edit or share new content. Full access returns as soon as the outstanding balance is paid. Past-due accounts are not eligible for refunds until the balance is paid, as described in the Refund Policy.

## Billing disputes

Customers who believe a charge is wrong should contact support with the invoice ID before raising a dispute with their bank. A disputed card charge, also called a chargeback, places the workspace on hold until the dispute is resolved. Suspected billing errors, such as a duplicate charge or a charge for users who were removed before the billing date, are reviewed by a billing specialist through a support ticket.
