## Cancelling a Nimbus workspace

A Nimbus workspace can be cancelled by its workspace owner. Cancelling stops future billing and, at the end of any paid period, closes the workspace. How and when a cancellation takes effect depends on the workspace's plan, as explained in the sections below. Cancelling a paid plan and deleting a Nimbus account are separate actions: cancelling stops the subscription, while account deletion permanently removes the account and needs a human specialist through a support ticket.

## Free plan {tier=free}

Free plan workspaces can be deleted at any time by the workspace owner from the workspace admin settings, under Workspace, then Delete workspace. Deleting a Free plan workspace takes effect immediately: all users lose access straight away. Any Free plan add-ons, such as extra storage, stop renewing when the workspace is deleted. Free plan charges are not eligible for refunds, so no partial refund is issued for unused add-on time. Free plan workspace data is kept for 30 days after deletion and then permanently deleted.

## Pro plan {tier=pro}

Pro plan monthly subscriptions cancel at the end of the current billing period. After a Pro plan monthly customer cancels, the workspace keeps full Pro features until the last day of the period that has already been paid for, and no further invoices are issued. The workspace then closes.

Pro plan annual subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, and the workspace keeps full Pro features until the end of the paid annual term. The remaining months of a Pro plan annual term are not refunded beyond what the Refund Policy allows. Customers on a Pro annual plan who want a refund when cancelling should check the Refund Policy for the Pro plan limit before cancelling.

Pro plan workspace owners can cancel from the Billing page in the workspace admin settings, or by opening a support ticket. A Pro plan customer can also choose to downgrade to the Free plan instead of cancelling, which keeps the workspace open within Free plan limits; downgrades are handled by a specialist through a support ticket.

## Enterprise plan {tier=enterprise}

Enterprise plan cancellations follow the terms of the customer's contract. Enterprise customers must give 30 days' written notice to cancel, sent by the account owner to their customer success manager or through a support ticket. The cancellation takes effect at the later of the end of the 30-day notice period or the end date set in the contract. Any fees committed in the contract remain payable unless the contract says otherwise. Enterprise refunds on cancellation follow the Refund Policy for the Enterprise plan. The customer success manager confirms the final access date and offers help with exporting data before the workspace closes.

## What happens to data after cancellation

When a Nimbus workspace closes after cancellation, all workspace data is kept for 30 days. During the 30-day retention period the workspace is read-only for the workspace owner, who can sign in to export files and the activity log. Other users lose access when the workspace closes. The workspace can be reactivated during the 30-day retention period by subscribing again, with all files and settings restored. After the 30-day retention period ends, all workspace data, including files, version history and activity logs, is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Data held in backups is removed on the normal backup rotation.

## Before cancelling

Nimbus recommends that workspace owners export their data before cancelling, using the steps in the data export troubleshooting guide. Owners should also disconnect integrations such as Slack, Google Drive and Microsoft 365, and revoke any API keys, so that external tools stop sending requests to the closed workspace.
