## How Nimbus support works

Nimbus support is available through the support assistant, email, live chat, phone and the community forum, depending on the workspace's plan. Response times below are first response times: the time from when a request is received to when a Nimbus support agent first replies. Resolution time depends on the issue. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays. Customers should include their account ID (for example, C-1001) in every request so support can find the workspace quickly.

## Severity levels

Nimbus classifies each support request by severity:

- Severity 1 (critical): the service is down or unusable for the whole workspace, or data loss is happening, with no workaround.
- Severity 2 (high): a core feature such as sync, sharing or sign-in is badly affected for many users, with no reasonable workaround.
- Severity 3 (normal): a feature is not working as expected, but a workaround exists or few users are affected.
- Severity 4 (low): questions, how-to requests and feature requests.

Severity levels affect response targets on the Enterprise plan. On Free and Pro plans, all requests use the plan's standard response target.

## Free plan {tier=free}

Free plan support is provided through the Nimbus community forum and by email. Free plan email requests receive a response within 3 business days. The community forum is monitored by Nimbus staff and other Nimbus users, and many common questions are already answered there. Free plan support does not include live chat or phone support, and Free plan requests are handled in the order received regardless of severity.

## Pro plan {tier=pro}

Pro plan support is provided by email and live chat, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm Eastern Time (ET). Pro plan requests receive a first response within 1 business day. Live chat is available from the help menu inside Nimbus during Pro plan support hours. Requests sent outside Pro plan support hours, including at weekends, are answered from the start of the next business day. Pro plan support does not include phone support or an uptime SLA.

## Enterprise plan {tier=enterprise}

Enterprise plan support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, by phone, chat and email. Enterprise first response targets depend on severity:

- Severity 1 (critical): first response within 1 hour, at any time of day.
- Severity 2 (high): first response within 4 hours, at any time of day.
- Severity 3 and 4: first response within 1 business day.

Severity 1 issues should be reported by phone for the fastest response. Every Enterprise customer has a named customer success manager who handles onboarding, quarterly reviews, escalations and contract questions.

### Enterprise uptime SLA and service credits

The Nimbus Enterprise plan includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. Uptime is measured each calendar month across the Nimbus web service, sync service and API, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance. If monthly uptime falls below the SLA, Enterprise customers are entitled to service credits:

- Below 99.9% uptime: a credit of 10% of that month's fees.
- Below 99.0% uptime: a credit of 25% of that month's fees.

Service credits are applied to the next Enterprise invoice and are not paid out as cash. To claim a service credit, the Enterprise customer must open a support ticket or contact their customer success manager before the end of the calendar month that follows the affected month. Service credits are the Enterprise customer's sole remedy for missed uptime unless the contract states otherwise.

## Escalating a support request

If a request is not progressing, customers can ask for it to be escalated by replying to the existing support ticket. Pro plan escalations are reviewed by a support team lead. Enterprise escalations are reviewed by a support team lead and the customer's customer success manager. The support assistant can open a support ticket on a customer's behalf whenever a request needs a human specialist.
