## Overview of Nimbus plans

Nimbus is a cloud workspace for storing, syncing and collaborating on files. Every Nimbus workspace is on one of three plans: Free, Pro or Enterprise. The plan determines storage, collaboration limits, API access, admin controls and the level of support the workspace receives. A workspace has exactly one plan at a time, and the plan applies to every user in that workspace.

Plan prices are as follows:

- Free: $0.
- Pro: $49 per user per month, or $490 per user per year when billed annually.
- Enterprise: priced per contract, billed monthly or annually.

Prices exclude applicable taxes, which are added to each invoice as described in the Billing Policy.

## Free plan {tier=free}

The Nimbus Free plan costs $0 and is intended for individuals and small groups trying Nimbus. The Free plan includes:

- Up to 3 users per workspace.
- 5 GB of total workspace storage, shared across all users.
- File sync across devices and web access.
- Shared folders and share links with view or edit permissions.
- 30 days of file version history.
- Data export, limited to 2 GB per export request.
- Support through the community forum and email, with a response within 3 business days.

The Free plan does not include API access, webhooks, SSO, admin audit logs or a choice of data region. Free plan workspaces can buy extra storage as an add-on; Free plan add-on charges are treated as Free plan charges under the Refund Policy.

## Pro plan {tier=pro}

The Nimbus Pro plan costs $49 per user per month, or $490 per user per year billed annually. Annual billing costs the equivalent of ten months of monthly billing. The Pro plan includes everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited users, billed per active user.
- 1 TB of storage per user, pooled across the workspace.
- 180 days of file version history and deleted-file recovery.
- Integrations with Slack, Google Drive and Microsoft 365.
- REST API and webhooks, with a rate limit of 100 API requests per minute.
- Two-factor authentication enforcement for all workspace members.
- Basic admin console with user management and sharing controls.
- Email and live chat support, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm ET, with a first response within 1 business day.

Pro plan seats are counted on the billing date. Adding users mid-cycle is charged on a prorated basis on the next invoice.

## Enterprise plan {tier=enterprise}

The Nimbus Enterprise plan is priced per contract and billed monthly or annually, with payment by card or by invoice on net-30 terms. The Enterprise plan includes everything in Pro, plus:

- Storage allocation set in the contract, with no per-user cap.
- Unlimited file version history.
- SSO through SAML 2.0 and automated user provisioning.
- Admin audit logs and advanced sharing policies.
- Choice of data residency region (US or EU).
- REST API and webhooks, with a rate limit of 1,000 API requests per minute.
- 24/7 support by phone, chat and email, with a 1-hour first response for critical (severity 1) issues.
- A named customer success manager.
- A 99.9% monthly uptime SLA backed by service credits.

Enterprise plan terms, including seat counts, storage and renewal dates, are set out in the customer's contract. Where the contract and this help centre differ, the contract applies.

## Changing plans

A workspace can move between Free, Pro and Enterprise at any time. Plan changes, both upgrades and downgrades, are handled by a human billing specialist through a support ticket; the support assistant cannot change a plan directly. When upgrading, the new plan takes effect immediately and the price difference is prorated for the rest of the billing period. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the end of the current billing period. If a workspace uses more storage or users than the lower plan allows, the workspace becomes read-only for new uploads until usage is brought within the new plan's limits.
